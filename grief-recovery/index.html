What This Helps With

Bring language, structure, and care to loss.

Grief is not only connected to death. It can come through divorce, family changes, transitions, broken trust, health challenges, loss of faith, and painful memories that keep showing up in everyday life.

Stamena4Life offers faith-centered grief recovery support for people ready to take the next honest step toward healing.

For Individuals

Support for people processing loss, disappointment, difficult memories, emotional wounds, or major life change.

For Couples

A space for couples who want to better understand grief, pain, communication, and repair inside their relationship.

For Parents

Practical guidance for parents and caregivers helping children process loss, change, and hard emotions.

For Groups

Options for churches, schools, ministries, and communities that need language and tools for collective healing.

Current session options: Troy, Michigan in-person groups and virtual Zoom cohorts.
